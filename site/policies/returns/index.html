Returns and refunds

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Supplements cannot be resold once a seal is broken, so returns are limited to unopened products and damaged deliveries.

Return window

You may request a return within 30 days of delivery, provided the seal is unopened and the packaging is undamaged.

Opened products cannot be returned for safety and hygiene reasons, even if most of the capsules remain.

Damaged or wrong item

If your parcel arrives damaged, leaking, or with the wrong product, email care@verda.in within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and clear photos of the parcel and the product.

We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping. You do not need to return the damaged item unless we ask for it.

How to start a return

Email care@verda.in with your order ID and the reason for the return.

We will confirm within one working day and share a pickup slot. Keep the product sealed and in its original carton until pickup.

Do not ship anything back without confirmation, because unclaimed parcels cannot be matched to an order.

Refunds

Once the returned product reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, the refund is initiated within 2 working days and usually reaches you within 5 working days.

Prepaid refunds go back to the original payment method. COD refunds are made by UPI or bank transfer to an account in the buyer's name.

Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is due to damage, a wrong item, or a manufacturing defect.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it is handed to the courier and receive a full refund.

Once dispatched, cancellation is treated as a return under this policy.

Non-returnable cases

Opened or used products, products without the original seal, and items damaged after delivery cannot be returned.

Orders flagged for repeated abuse of the returns process may be refused at our discretion.
