Product disclaimer

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Please read this before using any Verda product. It explains what our products are, and what they are not.

Not a medicine

Verda products are food supplements. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and they are not a substitute for a varied diet or prescribed medication.

Speak to a professional

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before use if you are pregnant, nursing, under 18, taking prescription medication, or managing a medical condition.

Do not stop or change prescribed treatment in favour of a supplement.

Dose and storage

Do not exceed the stated dose. More is not better and may be harmful.

Keep all products out of reach of children. Store below 30°C in a dry place, away from direct sunlight.

Allergens

Check the allergen line on every label before use. If you have a known allergy, contact care@verda.in for the full ingredient list of the current batch.

Individual results

Nutrition affects people differently. Reviews and testimonials describe individual experiences, not typical or guaranteed outcomes.

Imported products

Verda products are imported and sold under an FSSAI Central Licence (Importer). The importer details, country of origin and licence number are printed on the pack and listed in the footer of this site.

Reporting a problem

If you experience an adverse reaction, stop using the product and seek medical advice. Please also report it to care@verda.in so we can investigate the batch.
