Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you browse or order, why we need it, and the choices you have. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

Account data: name, email address, profile photo and account identifier from Google sign-in.

Order data: phone number, delivery address, items ordered, order value and payment method.

Support data: messages you send to care@verda.in or on WhatsApp.

Consent data: your cookie choices, the time you made them, and the source of the request.

Technical data: only if you allow analytics, we record pages viewed, scroll depth, device type and approximate performance metrics. We do not record keystrokes or form contents.

Why we use it

To accept and deliver orders, send invoices, and handle returns.

To provide support and respond to your questions.

To prevent fraud and misuse of cash on delivery.

To send marketing messages only if you opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Legal basis

We process order data to perform the contract you enter when you buy from us.

Analytics, marketing messages and optional cookies are based on your consent. Consent is not bundled with the terms of sale.

Who we share it with

Payment providers, to process prepaid payments and refunds.

Courier partners, to deliver your order and provide tracking.

Email and WhatsApp providers, to send order updates and, if you opt in, marketing.

Cloud infrastructure providers, including Google Firebase, which hosts our store and database.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose it to authorities only when required by law.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for 3 years to meet tax and accounting requirements.

Marketing consent records are kept until you withdraw consent, plus a short period to demonstrate compliance.

Support conversations are kept for 12 months.

Analytics data is retained in aggregate and cannot be used to identify you after 14 months.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of data we no longer need.

You can withdraw marketing consent at any time using the unsubscribe link or by writing to us.

We respond to requests within 30 days.

Grievance officer: care@verda.in, Verda Nutrition Pvt Ltd, Mumbai.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics and marketing tags are off until you accept them in the cookie banner.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing site data for this domain and choosing again.

Children

Our products and store are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email.
