Shipping policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how Verda ships orders within India, what delivery costs, and how cash on delivery works.

Where we ship

We currently ship within India only. International shipping is not available at launch.

If your PIN code is not serviceable, checkout will tell you before payment. We do not accept orders for addresses we cannot reach.

Dispatch time

Orders placed before 2:00 pm on a working day are packed and handed to the courier the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 pm, on Sundays, or on public holidays are dispatched the next working day.

You will receive a tracking link by email and WhatsApp once the courier scans the parcel.

Delivery estimates

Metro cities: usually 2 to 4 working days.

Rest of India: usually 4 to 7 working days.

Remote PIN codes and the festive season can add a few days. These are estimates, not guarantees, because the final leg is operated by third-party couriers.

Shipping charges

Free shipping on orders of ₹999 and above.

Orders below ₹999 carry a flat shipping fee of ₹59.

Prices include 5% GST. The shipping fee is shown at checkout before you place the order.

Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available on orders up to ₹2,000, with a ₹29 handling fee.

COD orders are confirmed by WhatsApp or OTP before dispatch. Unconfirmed orders are held for 24 hours and then cancelled.

COD may be unavailable for some PIN codes based on courier risk. If COD is not available for your address, prepaid payment will be the only option.

Failed delivery

The courier will attempt delivery twice. If both attempts fail, the parcel returns to our warehouse.

Prepaid orders that return to us are refunded minus the actual forward and return shipping cost. COD orders that return to us are not charged, but repeated refusals may lead to COD being disabled for that phone number.

Address changes

Contact care@verda.in as soon as possible. We can change the address only before the parcel is handed to the courier. Once dispatched, the address cannot be changed.
