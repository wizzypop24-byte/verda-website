Questions, answered

Safety, dosage, shipping and subscriptions.

Are these supplements safe to take every day?

All four formulas are made for daily use at the dose printed on the label. Every batch is tested by an independent laboratory before it ships, and the certificate of analysis is published on the product page. Do not exceed the stated dose. If you are pregnant, nursing, under 18, or taking prescription medication, speak to a qualified healthcare professional before starting. Supplements support normal body functions; they are not a substitute for a varied diet.

How do I know the dose is right?

Each product page lists the exact dose per serving, the form of the ingredient, and why that amount was chosen. We use the same amounts studied in published research rather than proprietary blends that hide individual quantities. If a formula contains more than one ingredient, every amount is printed separately. Your doctor can review the label with you, especially if you take other medication.

Can I take these with my medication?

Check with your doctor or pharmacist first. Some minerals and vitamins interact with prescription medicines, for example calcium and magnesium can affect certain antibiotics, and vitamin K can interact with blood thinners. Bring the label to your appointment so the dose and form are visible. We publish full ingredient lists so your clinician has the information they need.

How does shipping work?

Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same working day from our Mumbai warehouse. Delivery usually takes two to four working days for metros and four to seven days elsewhere. Shipping is free above ₹999; below that a flat ₹59 applies. Cash on delivery is available up to ₹2,000 with a ₹29 handling fee. You will receive tracking by email and WhatsApp.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days only if the seal is unopened, because supplements cannot be resold once the seal is broken. If a product arrives damaged or the wrong item was sent, contact care@verda.in with a photo and we will replace it at no cost. Refunds go back to the original payment method within five working days. Opened products cannot be returned for safety reasons.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Yes. Subscribe and save orders are 12% cheaper and renew monthly. You can skip a delivery, change the date, or cancel from your account at any time before the order is packed. There is no minimum commitment and no cancellation fee. If a formula is out of stock, we will email you before charging and hold the order until it is available.
