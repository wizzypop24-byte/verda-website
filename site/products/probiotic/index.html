Questions

Do I need to refrigerate it?

No. The strains were selected for room-temperature stability, but keep the bottle closed and away from humidity. If your home gets very hot, a cool cupboard is better than a kitchen counter.

When will I notice a difference?

Digestion varies from person to person. Give it a few weeks of consistent use, and speak to your doctor if symptoms persist or worsen.

Can I take it with antibiotics?

Ask your doctor. Many people space probiotics a few hours away from antibiotic doses, but the timing should be confirmed by your clinician.
