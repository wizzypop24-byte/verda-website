Questions

How long does biotin take?

Hair grows slowly, so give it at least two to three months of consistent use. Results vary, and a balanced diet matters more than any single supplement.

Does biotin affect blood tests?

Yes. High-dose biotin can interfere with some thyroid and hormone tests. Tell your doctor that you take it, and stop a few days before a blood test if advised.

Can I take it with a multivitamin?

Check the total biotin across products to avoid doubling up. If you already take a multivitamin, compare labels before adding this.
