Questions

Can I take magnesium at night?

Yes. Many people prefer it in the evening because it supports normal muscle relaxation. Take it with food to reduce the chance of loose stools, and keep to the stated dose.

Why glycinate instead of oxide?

Glycinate is a chelated form, which is generally gentler on the digestive system. Oxide is cheaper but has lower absorption and is more likely to cause loose stools at higher doses.

Is this safe with other medication?

Check with your doctor or pharmacist first. Magnesium can affect how certain antibiotics and thyroid medicines are absorbed, so timing may need to be adjusted.
