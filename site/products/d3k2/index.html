Questions

Why combine D3 with K2?

Vitamin D supports calcium absorption, and vitamin K2 supports normal calcium metabolism. Pairing them is common in daily formulas aimed at bone and immune support.

Do I need a blood test first?

It is worth asking your doctor. Vitamin D status is easy to test, and the right dose depends on your current level rather than a guess.

Can I take it with calcium?

Many people do, but confirm the total daily amount with your clinician so you stay within safe limits.
