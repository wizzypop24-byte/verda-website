Supplements that show their work

Verda exists because supplement labels are often hard to read on purpose. We print the dose, test the batch, and publish what we find.

Why we started

We kept picking up bottles that listed a “proprietary blend” instead of amounts. When a label hides the dose, you cannot compare it with the research, or with another brand. You are left trusting the loudest claim.

So we built the opposite: a small range of formulas where every ingredient and every amount is printed, and where the certificate of analysis for each batch is available before you buy.

Four rules we do not bend

Clinically dosed

We start from the amounts used in published research, not from the smallest amount that still allows a claim.

Third-party tested

Every batch is tested by an independent laboratory for potency, purity and heavy metals.

No fillers

No added sugar, no unnecessary excipients, nothing added just to make the capsule cheaper.

Plain language

No miracle claims, no fake countdowns, no before-and-after photos. Structure and function only.

Where our products come from

Our formulas are imported and sold in India under an FSSAI Central Licence (Importer). The importer name, registered address and country of origin are printed on every pack and listed in the footer of this site.

Because supplements are a high-risk food category, consignments clear through designated ports with sampling and laboratory testing. We also keep a product information file for every SKU, covering the formulation, ingredient schedules and shelf-life data.

What we will not do

We will not claim that a supplement treats or cures a disease. We will not invent reviews, pay for star ratings, or use a doctor’s face to imply an endorsement we do not have. If a claim needs a study, we link the study.

What you can check before you buy

  • Full ingredient list with doses and forms on every product page
  • Certificate of analysis linked per batch
  • FSSAI licence number and importer details in the footer
  • Shipping, returns, privacy and terms written in plain language
